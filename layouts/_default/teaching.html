PHYS 111: Quantum Mechanics and You

Course design, Graduate Teaching and Learning Program Level 3, January–April 2022

For Level 3 of the program, Benjamin designed and outlined a university course in quantum mechanics for incoming first-year students from all over campus, organized in modules around quantum principles and their applications without heavy mathematical formalism. He presented the course design at the NanoCanada From Earth to Space conference in June 2022.
